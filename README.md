# CCST Cybersecurity Study Course

A beginner-friendly, in-depth study guide for the **Cisco Certified Support Technician (CCST) Cybersecurity** exam. It covers all 5 objective domains and 22 objectives, explained in plain English, with practice questions after every objective and an answer key for each domain.

> **Unofficial study aid.** This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cisco or Certiport. It is based on the published CCST Cybersecurity exam objectives. Always confirm current exam details on Cisco's official exam page.

## What's Inside

- **Plain-English explanations** of each objective, so you get the big picture first
- **Deep dives** with the precise details the exam expects (tables, port numbers, commands, frameworks)
- **Practice questions** after every objective
- **Answer keys** at the end of each domain
- **Master reference list** of videos, websites, standards, and tools
- **6-week study plan**, hands-on lab ideas, and exam tips
- **PDF bookmarks** for fast navigation between domains and objectives

| # | Domain | Objectives |

| 5 | Incident Handling | 5.1 – 5.4 |

## Topics You Will Learn

- **Principles:** CIA triad, risk vocabulary, attacker types, AAA, MFA, encryption, hashing, PKI
- **Network security:** TCP/IP weaknesses, IPv4/IPv6, CIDR, NAT, DMZ, IDS/IPS, secure Wi-Fi, ACLs, firewalls, VPN, NAC
- **Endpoints:** OS security, `netstat`, `nslookup`, `tcpdump`, patching, log analysis, malware removal
- **Risk management:** vulnerability scanning, CVE/CVSS, risk ranking, disaster recovery and business continuity
- **Incident handling:** SIEM/SOAR, Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATT&CK, Diamond Model, chain of custody, compliance frameworks, NIST incident response lifecycle

## Getting Started

1. Open `CCST_Cybersecurity_Study_Course.pdf`.
2. Work through one domain at a time, in order.
3. Try the **Check yourself** questions *before* looking at the answer key.
4. Do the hands-on labs listed in the final chapter to lock in the concepts.

## Suggested Study Plan

| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Domain 1: build vocabulary with flashcards |
| 2 | Domain 2: CIDR math, ACL reading, Packet Tracer lab |
| 3 | Domain 3: command-line tools and Linux permissions in a VM |
| 4 | Domain 4: browse the NVD, read a CVE, scan your own lab |
| 5 | Domain 5: map a real attack to the Kill Chain and ATT&CK |
| 6 | Mixed practice, then redo everything you missed |

## Ethical Use

Only scan, capture traffic on, or test systems you **own or have written permission** to test. Practice in your own lab or in legal training environments.
